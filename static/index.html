How to use

Direct all questions and feedback to cityblock on Discord.

Select a tile type from the right sidebar.

Click the map’s tiles to change them.

Click the + on each side to expand the map.

Use UNDO and CLEAR buttons when needed.

Use the tabs at the top for independent maps. Import opens the map in a new tab with no undo history and no calculation preview. Export writes this tab as Polytopia Board Notation. Sessions are saved in this browser.

Click Calculate to find the best possible market layouts.

Each result is a trade-off between market stars, building population and border growths used; pick one to preview it. A result may ask for border growths the map does not have yet — those cities are outlined in teal, with the borders they would gain.

To use for buildings/resources other than windmills/farms, make the following substitutions:
Math: Windmill -> Sawmill, Forest -> Crop, Lumber Hut -> Farm
Smithery: Windmill -> Forge, Metal -> Crop, Mine -> Farm

To upgrade cities:

Select the Upgrade tool in the sidebar.
Click a village to turn it into a small city (3×3 border).
Click a small city to expand to a big city (5×5 border).
Polytopia rules are upheld (cities which expand earlier get contested tiles).
Existing cities cannot be overwritten with other tile tools, and you must Undo or Clear them.

What this can and can't do

Can:
Given a map and a city capture/border growth order, find the market layouts that trade off market stars, building population and border growths used, for a single resource/building pair.
It also tries every combination of extra border growths up to a third of your cities, so it will tell you which cities are worth growing.
Can't: (for now)
Try more border growths than a third of your cities, or reorder the ones you already made
Use 2 or more resources/building pairs (sorry xin)
Optimize for anything else (like hanging population)
